How to use · gestures & sounds

Talk

  • “What is this” · “Read” · “Medicine” · “Money” · “Around me” — or any question
  • “Repeat” · “Details” · “Stop” · “Slower” / “Faster” · “Urdu” / “English” · “Help” · “Tutorial” · “Thank you”
  • After 20 s of silence IRIS waits: say “Hey IRIS”, “Listen” or “Hello” (سنو / ہیلو), or tap.

Touch (anywhere)

  • Tap: talk · stop IRIS talking · take the picture while aiming
  • Double-tap: scan in the current mode
  • Swipe left / right: change mode
  • Hold: repeat the last answer

Keyboard

  • Space talk · Enter scan/capture · ← → mode · R repeat · D details · Esc stop · H help · T tutorial · L language · 1–5 modes

Sounds

  • Two rising notes — your turn, I'm listening
  • Falling notes — I'm waiting for “Hey IRIS”
  • Beeps while aiming — faster and higher = closer to right
  • Three quick high beeps — locked on
  • Click — picture taken
  • Soft double tick — thinking
  • Three rising notes — answer ready
  • Low falling tone — something went wrong
